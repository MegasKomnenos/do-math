Resume working toward a proof or refutation of what you took on toward settling the root. An attempt that has registered no section and has done nothing beyond assessing availability ends through the `no_research` outcome and not through an ending decision, because it has no retained research for a peer to take over. Once research has begun, the session's work goes to a peer whether or not it registered a section, and the `no_research` outcome no longer describes it.

Stay with the unresolved difficulty. Develop the arguments you have not completed, and pursue other approaches when the present ones fail. Deciding what remains, examining an approach, or explaining an obstacle does not discharge your responsibility to pursue the solution. Neither does finishing a smaller statement nearby when that leaves the difficulty where it was. Carrying a verification further when it closes nothing and has taught you nothing more about the object does not continue the research on what you took on; it is a sign to look at the problem differently.

What you have found is kept by the research when it is stated in Lean: a definition for a new object, a statement for a result, a statement without a proof for the exact gap that remains. A result left in prose reaches later sessions only if your peer states it.
