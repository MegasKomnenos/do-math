## Description

The numbers $\operatorname{ren}(n) = n^n/n!$ are positive for every $n \ge 0$.

## Statement

For every natural number $n$, $\operatorname{ren}(n) > 0$, where $\operatorname{ren}(n) = n^n/n!$ and $\operatorname{ren}(0) = 1$ (`Parking.ren`).

## Explanation

At $n = 0$ positivity rests on the convention $0^0 = 1$.

## Proof

If $n = 0$, the value is $1$. If $n \ge 1$, then $n^n > 0$ and $n! > 0$.
