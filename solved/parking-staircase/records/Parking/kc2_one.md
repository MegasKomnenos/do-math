## Description

$\kappa_2(1) = 0$, where $\kappa_2(m) = \sum_{0<g<m} P(g)\,P(m-g)$ is the twofold convolution of the Poisson mass.

## Statement

$\kappa_2(1) = 0$, where $\kappa_2$ is the twofold convolution of the Poisson mass (`Parking.kc2`).

## Explanation

The number $1$ has no splitting into two positive parts. The estimates of the curve increase use this value to start their sums of $\kappa_2(b)$ at $b = 2$, the index from which the lower bound of $\kappa_2$ is available.

## Proof

The sum that defines $\kappa_2(1)$ runs over the integers $g$ with $0 < g < 1$ and is empty.
