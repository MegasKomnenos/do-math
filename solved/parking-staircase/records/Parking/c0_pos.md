## Description

The constant $c_0 = 1/\sqrt{2\pi}$ is positive.

## Statement

$c_0 > 0$, where $c_0 = 1/\sqrt{2\pi}$ (`Parking.c0`).

## Explanation

Used where an inequality is multiplied or divided by $c_0$.

## Proof

$2\pi > 0$, so $\sqrt{2\pi} > 0$ and its reciprocal is positive.
