## Description

The coefficient of $X^n$ in the series $P$ is $c_n = (n+1)^{n-1}/n!$.

## Statement

For every natural number $n$, $[X^n]\,P = c_n$, where $P$ is the series of `Parking.TreeSeries.Pser` and $c_n = (n+1)^{n-1}/n!$ (`Parking.cc`).

## Explanation

This restates the definition of $P$ as the series with the coefficients $c_n$, in the form in which coefficient comparisons use it.

## Proof

By the definition of $P$.
