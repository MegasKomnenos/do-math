## Description

The tilted free prime kernel at the index $2$ is $\varepsilon_2 x^2 = x^2/2$.

## Statement

For every real number $x$, $\varepsilon_2\, x^2 = x^2/2$, where $\varepsilon_m = (m-1)^{m-1}/m!$ (`Parking.eps`) and $\varepsilon_m x^m$ is the tilted free prime kernel (`Parking.epsT`).

## Explanation

Like `Parking.epsT_one`, this explicit value is used in the left half, in a comparison of two values of the tilted prime kernel on the free range.

## Proof

By `Parking.eps_succ`, $\varepsilon_2 = 1^1/2! = 1/2$.
