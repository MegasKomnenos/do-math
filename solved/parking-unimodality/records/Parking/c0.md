## Description

The constant $c_0 = 1/\sqrt{2\pi} \approx 0.39894$ of Stirling's formula.

## Statement

$$c_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}.$$

## Explanation

Stirling's formula in the form used here says that the Poisson mass $e^{-n}n^n/n!$ (`Parking.poissonMass`) is close to $c_0/\sqrt{n}$; the two-sided bound with this constant is `Parking.poissonMass_le_c0` and `Parking.poissonMass_ge_c0`. Every explicit estimate of the library that goes back to Stirling's formula carries the constant $c_0$, and the final numerical comparisons use its decimal enclosure $0.39894 \le c_0 \le 0.39895$ (`Parking.c0_bounds`). The constant is a definition of its own so that the closed-form bounds of the crossing argument and of the far rows can be written with one symbol and share one enclosure.

The symbol $c_0$ is not the value at $n = 0$ of the sequence $c_n = (n+1)^{n-1}/n!$ of `Parking.cc`, which is $1$; the two are unrelated, and the Lean names `Parking.c0` and `Parking.cc 0` are distinct. A text that uses both needs another symbol for one of them.
