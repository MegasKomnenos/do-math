## Description

The Cayley coefficient of index $1$ is $a_1 = 1$.

## Statement

$a_1 = 1$, where $a_j = j^{j-1}/j!$ for $j \ge 1$ is the Cayley coefficient (`Parking.cay`).

## Explanation

The value of the sequence at its first nonzero index: $1^0/1! = 1$.

## Proof

Unfold the definition at $j = 1$.
