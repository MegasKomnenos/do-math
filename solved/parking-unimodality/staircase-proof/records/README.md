# The records of the proof of unimodality through the staircase

This folder holds written records for the Lean proof in [`../lean/`](../lean/), which proves the unimodality of parking functions by maximum displacement through a stronger statement, the staircase. A record is a text for one public declaration of a Lean library: its statement in ordinary mathematical language, an explanation, and, for a theorem, its proof in words.

The proof rests on 445 public declarations: the 106 of the package `../lean/`, and 339 of the library `Parking` in [`../../lean/`](../../lean/), on which the package is built. (With the private declarations behind them it rests on 1,251, as `../lean/README.md` reports; records are written for public declarations only.) This folder holds 249 records, each at the path that follows the declaration's name (`Parking/staircase.md` for `Parking.staircase`). They are of two kinds.

- **107 records written for the package**: one for each of its 106 public declarations, and `Parking/unimodal_via_staircase_proof.md`. The record of the staircase, `Parking/staircase.md`, tells the proof as a whole. The paragraph after this list says why the unimodality theorem has two records.
- **142 records of declarations of `Parking`**, taken from [`../../records/`](../../records/), the records of that library, with their explanations adjusted so that they say how this proof uses the statement. The uses were read from the compiled library. The statements and proofs of these records are those of `../../records/`, with two exceptions, which were made so that no record names a declaration outside the 445. In `Parking/b.md` the statement says in words what a parking function is, where the record in `../../records/` names the definition `Parking.IsParkingFunction`, which is not among them. In `Parking/Tq_ratio.md` one sentence of the proof was removed, because it named two declarations that are not among them.

The unimodality theorem has two records because the records follow a packaging of the sources in which the runtime of a research system compiled the proof, which [`../lean/README.md`](../lean/README.md) describes under "What was checked, and how". In the package the theorem is `Parking.unimodal_via_staircase`, proved in `../lean/Staircase/Main.lean`. In the packaged library that name belongs to the unimodality statement posed as the proposition to be proved, with its proof left open, and the proved theorem is carried under the name `Parking.unimodal_via_staircase_proof`, with the identical Lean statement. Accordingly `Parking/unimodal_via_staircase.md` is the record of the proposition as it was posed: it has no proof, and it reads the Lean statement, part by part, against the conjecture as it is stated in words. `Parking/unimodal_via_staircase_proof.md` is the record of the proved theorem, with the proof and, in its section "Earlier work", an account of the literature. A reader who comes from the package therefore finds the proof of its theorem `Parking.unimodal_via_staircase` in the second of these two files.

The other 197 declarations of `Parking` on which the proof rests have their records in `../../records/`, and those records are not repeated here. They are 194 files, because the constructor and the two fields of the structure `Parking.IsWSol` have no record of their own. The bibliography of all the records is `../../records/bibliography.bib`.

## How the records were made and checked

The 107 records for the package were written by several AI sessions from the Lean proofs, private lemmas included. Every one of them was compared with the Lean, statement by statement and proof by proof, by a session that had not written it. A record that was changed afterwards, because a proof of the package was simplified or because the library `Parking` was corrected, was compared again by such a session: whole where its statement or its proof had changed, and otherwise in the sentences that had changed. The comparisons corrected the records where they differed from the Lean and where a remark in an explanation was not true. Figures from floating-point evaluations that explanations quote were recomputed by the comparing sessions or by a separate session, and are marked in the records as not part of the proof. What the section "Earlier work" of `Parking/unimodal_via_staircase_proof.md` says of a work of the literature is what the corresponding section of `../../records/Parking/root_unimodal_proof.md` says of it; the works were not read again for this folder. The statements and proofs of the 142 records of `Parking` declarations are those of `../../records/` and were not compared with the Lean again here.

Apart from seventeen of them, the records were also read whole by sessions that had written or adjusted none of them. These sessions checked every explanation against the account of the whole proof in `Parking/staircase.md` and against the uses read from the compiled library, and corrected the sentences that did not agree with them. Thirteen of the seventeen are the account of the proof and the records closest to it: `Parking/staircase.md`, the two records of the unimodality theorem, and the records of `column_rule`, `diagonal_rule`, `diagonal_rule_small`, `step_pos_beyond`, `curve_crossing`, `positive_row_early`, `one_sign_change`, `step_persists`, `step_persists_iter` and `step_pos_large`. In place of that reading, a session that had written none of the thirteen compared each of them whole with the Lean, including what rests on what. The other four did not exist in their present form at that reading (`Parking/Kpoly_second_diff.md`, `Parking/curvSum_eq_sum.md`, `Parking/factorial_lt_robbins.md`, `Parking/renewal_logConvex.md`); they were compared with their sources as the previous paragraph says. A script confirms that the 443 records, the 249 here and the 194 in `../../records/`, are present and well formed, and that none of them names a declaration other than the 445 and the proved theorem `Parking.unimodal_via_staircase_proof`.

No person has read the records.
