1"""Knowledge candidates, their synthetic formal evidence and small knowledge states.
2
3Compiler results here are explicit doubles (``formal_result``), never a Lean
4run; identity assessments are recorded directly, never reviewed.
5
6- ``knowledge_candidate(**changes)``: the theorem candidate "Truth"
7 (``theorem truth : True := True.intro``); ``changes`` replace its fields.
8- ``formal_result(source, declarations, toolchain, ...)``: a verified compiler
9 result for ``source``; every declaration depends on ``closure`` and carries
10 the given dependency and declaration evidence strings.
11- ``formal_evidence(state, kid, env='TE-old')``: ``formal_result`` for the
12 stored formal record of ``kid`` under trust environment ``env``.
13- ``seal_knowledge(state, runtime, kid, env='TE-old')``: record that evidence and a
14 matching identity assessment; returns ``(state, assessment ID)``.
15- ``add_knowledge(state, runtime, *, verified=True, **changes)``: import a
16 candidate and seal it; returns ``(state, runtime, kid, assessment ID)``.
17- ``knowledge_graph()``: ``(state, runtime, ids)``, three sealed records ``a``,
18 ``b`` (depends on ``a``) and ``c`` with a relation ``a``→``c``, a current goal
19 judgment using ``b`` and completed tasks using ``b`` and ``c``.
20- ``impact_declaration(state, runtime, roots)``: the complete computed impact a
21 producer declares; ``revise_knowledge`` revises one record with it.
22- ``activate_kind(state, token, family='relation', definition=None)``: register,
23 check and activate one kind definition.
24- ``verified_knowledge(environment=None, *, binding=None, identity=True)``:
25 ``(state, runtime, kid, assessment ID)``, the candidate verified under
26 ``TE-test`` and (unless ``identity=False``) assessed as a match.
27- ``formal_boundary_engine(workspace, toolchain=None)``: ``(engine, kid)``, a
28 bare engine with an adopted trust environment ``TE-old`` (the installed
29 ``toolchain`` when given, else a synthetic one) and one unverified candidate
30 whose formal check replaces admission, storage and review start with autospec
31 doubles; its verifier returns ``formal_evidence``.
32"""

49def formal_result(source, declarations, toolchain, *, closure=('True', 'True.intro'),
50 dependency_evidence=('fixture-type', 'fixture-value'),
51 declaration_evidence=('fixture-type', 'fixture-proof'), **extra):
52 """A verified compiler result for ``source``; ``extra`` adds fields such as ``allowed_axioms``."""
53 return dict(status='verified', source_sha256=hashlib.sha256(source.encode()).hexdigest(),
54 declarations=list(declarations), toolchain=deepcopy(toolchain), **extra,
55 axioms={n: [] for n in declarations}, dependency_closure={n: list(closure) for n in declarations},
56 dependency_evidence={n: dict(zip(closure, dependency_evidence)) for n in declarations},
57 declaration_evidence={n: {'type': declaration_evidence[0], 'value': declaration_evidence[1]}
58 for n in declarations})
