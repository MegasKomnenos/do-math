1"""The purpose scenario: a scripted research run that proves one natural-number identity.
2
3``PurposeRunner(scenario='direct', observation=None)`` answers every role from
4its actual input: preparation proposes task ``A`` (a formal theorem, ``SOURCE``),
5review approves, work returns ``theorem()``, identity review matches and the
6goal audit is satisfied once a certified result exists. ``scenario`` selects
7variants (``dependency``: ``B`` before ``A``; ``dispute``: one withdrawn
8objection; ``currentness``: a downstream task that consumes the result;
9``catalog``: a long report); ``observation`` drives live observation of the
10first work call (``continue``, ``stop``, ``external_pause``). ``formal_fixture``
11is its compiler double, which covers ``SOURCE`` only. ``parse_context``,
12``common``, ``candidate`` and ``theorem`` are the pieces of its answers;
13``GOAL``, ``STATEMENT``, ``EXPLANATION`` and ``NEW_GOAL`` are its texts.
14``AcademicRunner`` extends it with academic tasks, resolutions and external
15effects; ``CheckedRunner`` wraps a runner so that its output passes the
16producer check and control transport as a worker's does.
17
18Harnesses
19---------
20``PurposeHarness`` holds the scenario's configuration (``fixture_config``) and
21``drive(engine, predicate=None, limit=45)``, which steps the engine until
22``predicate`` (default: the goal is mathematically solved) and fails the test
23if the flow stops first. ``ActivatedPurposeHarness`` hands out template copies
24(``make``: the public activation; ``make_active(**config)``: the validated
25synthetic active state), one live copy at a time.
26
27Template builds (``research_fixtures.restore_template``)
28--------------------------------------------------------
29- ``solved_instance(root, shared, *, criteria)``: the public activation driven to
30 the mathematical solution; ``criteria`` approves requirement ``writeup``
31 (``requirement``) first. It returns the instance ID, the head the driving
32 engine published last and the runner's work order. ``drive_to`` is the
33 build's bounded drive.
34- ``purpose_state(root, shared, *, config, until, ...)``: the synthetic active
35 state driven until task ``A``'s work is queued (``until='prepared'``) or its
36 identity review opens (``'review'``); ``purpose_copy`` gives a harness test
37 an engine on a copy.
38- ``assessment_preparation_instance(root, shared, *, config)``: two academic
39 tasks awaiting model assessment; ``prepared_assessment_engine`` gives a
40 harness test an engine on a copy with ``ASSESSMENT_CONFIG``.
41``CURRENTNESS_CONFIG`` is the configuration of the currentness scenarios.
42"""
