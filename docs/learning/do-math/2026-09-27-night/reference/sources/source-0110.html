1"""Engine doubles and the engine start states tests share.
2
3Two kinds of engine appear here. Both are the product ``ResearchEngine``:
4
5- In-memory engines hold supplied state and publish nothing to Git.
6 ``FakeEngine(root, config=None)`` is an initialized instance ``RI-test`` over
7 the ``StateStore`` double whose ``_save`` records copies in ``saves``; it
8 grants, registers synthetic trust environments, activates through the
9 adoption decision (``activate``), creates knowledge and approved tasks, and
10 processes one queued machine action (``machine``). ``approve_contract`` and
11 ``formal_verification`` are its contract review and compiler double for
12 records outside it; ``formal_check_engine(test)`` is one with a candidate and
13 its queued formal check. ``routing_engine()`` is a bare engine with an empty
14 active-looking instance for response routing; ``intent_import`` and
15 ``intent_verdict`` author and check one interpretation on it.
16 ``context_engine(instance=None, runtime=None)`` keeps the real role-context
17 producer over ``record_fixtures.surface_state``; ``package`` prepares one
18 role input with it. ``initial_failure_engine`` holds a failed initial
19 interpretation waiting for maintenance. ``reassessment_engine`` holds a task
20 waiting for a missing input. ``governance_host`` and ``goal_audit_host`` hold
21 checked governance changes. ``evidence_engine`` is an active instance whose
22 store is ``input_snapshot_store``; ``reviewed_task``, ``apply_plan``,
23 ``accept_knowledge`` and ``audit_goal`` drive it.
24- Git-backed start states give a ``unittest.TestCase`` its attributes.
25 ``start_submission_history(test)`` puts a running maintenance execution with
26 a sealed input on a copy of the validated active state
27 (``state_fixtures.start_active_copy``); ``submit_history`` delivers its
28 submission intent and ``start_native_final`` adds its decision basis.
29 ``start_query_repository(test)`` publishes knowledge records and a relation
30 through the public ``initialize``. ``start_repair_host(test)`` gives a bare
31 engine over a real repository that holds one pending operational retry.
32 ``initialized`` and the template build ``initialized_instance`` run the
33 public ``initialize`` alone; ``PublicInitializationRunner`` answers its
34 interpretation roles.
35
36``native_result`` is a provider's native final output for a registered
37execution, as the runner reports it; it creates no process.
