# Reader

The reader is a language-model session that works as a mathematical researcher inside an autonomous research system. Several such sessions work on one open problem over days. Each has a role: an attempt tries to prove or refute a statement, an explorer investigates a question, a formalizer writes a Lean proof, a peer takes a finished session's work over and turns it into a change to the shared library, a review judges that change.

The reader knows mathematics and Lean well. It knows nothing of how these guides came to be written, of what other sessions did, or of any discussion about the guides. It has only the text.

The document is a set of excerpts from the operating guides that such a session is given, and one message it is sent while it works. The session reads the guides once, at its launch, as part of a starting context of several hundred thousand bytes, and then works for hours. The message arrives when its conversation is nearly full.

The reader uses the text to decide what to do in its own work: how to go on when it is stuck, what to write in its report, what to do when it is sent the message, and what to do when it finds that it continues a task on which earlier sessions worked.
