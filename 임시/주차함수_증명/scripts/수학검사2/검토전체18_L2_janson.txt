                                                Abstract. We study positive random variables whose moments can be
                                                expressed by products and quotients of Gamma functions; this includes
                                                many standard distributions. General results are given on existence,
                                                series expansion and asymptotics of density functions. It is shown that
                                                the integral of the supremum process of Brownian motion has moments
                                                of this type, as well as a related random variable occuring in the study
                                                of hashing with linear displacement, and the general results are applied
                                                to these variables.

   Several well-known distributions have moments or moment generating
functions of Gamma type. We give a number of examples in Section 3.

   The main motivation for the present paper is that also several less well-
known distributions have moments of Gamma type. It is then straightfor-
ward to use Mellin transform techniques to obtain expansions or asymptotics
of the density function, and it seems advantageous to do so, and to study
other properties, in general for this class of distributions.
