SV ANTE JANSON
Abstract. We study positive random variables whose moments can be
expressed by products and quotients of Gamma functions; thi s includes
many standard distributions. General results are given on e xistence,
series expansion and asymptotics of density functions. It i s shown that
the integral of the supremum process of Brownian motion has m oments
of this type, as well as a related random variable occuring in the study
of hashing with linear displacement, and the general result s are applied
to these variables.
